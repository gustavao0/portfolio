How my life changed using and studying english every single day

Learning English has become one of the most important experiences in my life. At first, I thought learning a second language was mainly about studying grammar and memorizing vocabulary, but over time, I realized that English is much more than a language. It has become a bridge between me and the rest of the world. My first language is Portuguese, and it is an important part of my identity and culture. However, English has allowed me to access information, communicate with people from different countries, and discover perspectives that I probably would not have experienced otherwise. I can watch videos, listen to podcasts, read articles, take courses, and learn from people all around the world. English has expanded my knowledge and made me more curious about different subjects. It has also improved my communication skills because I have learned that communication is not only about knowing many words. It is about expressing ideas clearly, listening carefully, asking questions, and understanding other people's perspectives. Sometimes, speaking English is still difficult for me. I can understand conversations, but when I need to speak, I sometimes freeze and forget words that I already know. However, I am learning that mistakes are part of the process. Every conversation gives me an opportunity to practice, improve, and become more confident.

Another amazing part of learning English is meeting people from other countries. I have made new friends who have different cultures, experiences, and ways of thinking. These relationships have taught me that there isn't only one way to see the world. When we communicate with people from different cultures, we become more open-minded. We start asking questions, becoming curious, and understanding that our own way of living is only one perspective among many. English has also changed the way I see my future. I can imagine working with international clients, communicating with companies from other countries, and creating opportunities that are not limited by geographical borders. For me, English is no longer just a subject that I study. It is a tool for personal growth, professional development, communication, and connection. I still have a lot to improve, especially my speaking, pronunciation, vocabulary, and spontaneity, but I know that every conversation brings me one step closer to becoming the person I want to be. Learning English has not only taught me how to speak another language; it has taught me how to see the world from different perspectives.This has provided me with massive cultural leverage, allowing me to connect and build meaningful friendships with people from different countries, such as the UK and the US.

Furthermore, it completely changed the way I study; by doing my research in English, I started acquiring deeper knowledge and genuinely understanding the ecosystem of people who live this in practice—the act of truly living and speaking another language.

This is exactly where I am certain that English changed my life.
